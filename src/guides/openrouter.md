## **How to Get an OpenRouter API Key**

OpenRouter is an incredible service. Instead of making you subscribe to OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google separately, OpenRouter acts as a single hub. It provides one API key that lets you talk to hundreds of different AI models—including many excellent free ones\!

Because you are using FrugaLLM, you don't even need to hunt down which models are free; FrugaLLM will handle finding and routing to the best free models for you automatically. You just need to provide the key.

### **Step 1: Create an Account**

1. Go to [https://openrouter.ai/](https://openrouter.ai/)  
2. Click on **Sign up** (usually in the top right corner).  
3. You can sign up using your Google account, MetaMask wallet, or a standard email and password.

### **Step 2: Generate Your API Key**

1. Once logged in, click on your profile icon in the top right corner.  
2. Select **Keys** from the drop-down menu.  
3. Click the **Create Key** button.  
4. Give your key a recognizable name (e.g., "FrugaLLM Setup").  
5. Click **Create**.  
6. **CRITICAL STEP**: A dialog box will appear with your API key. **Copy this string of text immediately**. OpenRouter will *never* show you this full key again for security reasons. Paste it straight into your FrugaLLM app or somewhere safe temporarily.

**💡 Pro Tip: Unlock Higher Limits for Free Models for $11**

While OpenRouter offers fantastic free models, completely free accounts are subject to strict "rate limits" (how many messages you can send per minute or hour). Because of how payment processors work, adding **$10 to $11** in credits to your OpenRouter account will instantly upgrade your account tier. This drastically increases your usage limits for those *free* models, ensuring you rarely hit a wall when chatting or generating content. (As a bonus, those credits will sit there in case you ever decide to test out a paid model\!).

### **Common Mistakes & Troubleshooting**

* **Lost Key**: If you lose your API key, don't panic. You cannot recover it, but you can simply delete the old one in the "Keys" menu and generate a new one.  
* **Format Errors**: When pasting your key into applications, ensure you don't accidentally include any extra spaces at the beginning or end of the text string.
